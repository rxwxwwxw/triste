# R03 — narrow invalidation experiment and stop gate

Hypothesis: retaining completed inner expressions that do not depend on a growing
outer seed can reduce nested recognition re-evaluation. R02 also requires cached
transitive dependencies; direct-hit-only filtering is unsound.

Prototype boundary: an explicit version and context opt-in; recognition only;
selective memoization; a whole grammar proven by existing analysis to have no
unknown/opaque calls or actions, no parameters/predicates, and one non-nullable recursive
rule. All other grammars, tree/record modes, trace callbacks, finite operational
limits and unproven entries keep broad invalidation. The minimum codegen proof
handshake may require grammar.d in addition to peg.d; no defaults or grammar
acceptance change. Do not silently trust an arbitrary caller's purity assertion.

Prototype tags each memo result with a conservative minimum seed dependency
position, propagates through active callers and completed hits, and filters only
proven independent completed entries. Active seeds must never be retired; retire
operations still unlink both lists and buckets. Measure the cost of the retained
entry list as well as avoided re-evaluation.

Checks: compare opt-in/off status, end and exact failEnd over direct, indirect,
mutual, nullable, nested and action/opaque fallback fixtures; full output, CTFE,
tracing and finite budgets via the differential corpus. Time optimized non-metric
builds in three runs × seven samples. Count work separately. Include flat chains,
deep nesting and a flat sequence of independent nested expressions.

Promotion requires repeated intended-case improvement with no unexplained major
regression and complete semantic proof. A proof gap or no net benefit means stop
and roll back production changes, retaining a reviewable patch and evidence as
an explicitly rejected experiment. Do not rewrite the memo table to rescue it.

Outcome: rejected after complete experiment; quadratic sibling-list scans outweigh
the nesting win. Source prototype stays archived; production remains R02. See
performance/results/R03/README.md for reproducible patch, tests and raw evidence.
