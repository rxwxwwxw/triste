# H02 — rule-local effects for reporting

Compute known/unknown and conservative actionful facts per rule, propagating
through every understood call, including cycles and calls after consumed input.
Opaque and parameterized entries are unknown and potentially actionful. Explicit
action wrappers retain their operand's call dependencies. Pure closed cycles can
be structurally known; known does not prove termination or acyclicity.

Use a fresh diagnostic graph only when a report is requested. Retain the existing
analysis graph, scalar/direct predicates and memo policy. Report facts must not
become optimization predicates in this milestone. H03/H04 are separate tickets.

Test first: unrelated pure islands, transitive opaque/action callers, contaminated
cycles, pure cycles, nested action operands, predicates, parameterized grammars/
rules and textual action-like literals. Keep H01's 72 generated-source goldens
unchanged. Add source-adjacent analysis units and bounded DMD/LDC integration;
verify >90% source coverage, differential, -vgc, sanitizer and Memcheck gates.

Outcome: rule-local known/potential-action facts are reported without changing
scalar/direct predicates or memo policy. DMD/LDC and optimized LDC agree; all 72
pre-H01 generated-source goldens still match. Source coverage exceeds 90% in every
executable module. Differential, -vgc, ASan/LSan, Memcheck and forced-GC retention
checks pass. See ../results/H02/README.md for the proof boundary and configuration
limits. H03/H04 remain separate policy changes.
